In Cornell’s words

  • “Not a crisis service”

    On its own advocates, who serve “members of the Cornell community.”1

  • “Off campus resource for students, staff, and faculty”

    On the Advocacy Center’s 24-hour hotline.2

  • “Independent of Cornell”

    On the Advocacy Center. We found no published Cornell funding for it.3

Cornell’s reported figures

  • Rapes on campus reported under federal law: 28 in 2023, 23 in 2024, 6 in 2025.3
  • In Cornell’s 2025 survey, 35% of undergraduate women reported nonconsensual sexual contact, up from 23% in 2023. Only 15% responded, and Cornell says to read the results with caution.4
  • On October 13, 2026, Cornell announced added victim advocate support on campus.5

Method

Cornell’s own published sources. No individuals named. Not affiliated with the Advocacy Center.
