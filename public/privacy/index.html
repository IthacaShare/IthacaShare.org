Privacy

This site counts visits without cookies. It never asks for your name, email, or anything else about you.

What we measure

Every page uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a small script that reports each page view to Cloudflare. It does not use cookies, and Cloudflare says it “does not collect or use your visitors’ personal data.” We see totals only: page views and visits, which page was viewed, the site that linked to it, country, browser, operating system, device type, and how quickly the page loaded.

Hosting

Cloudflare hosts this site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and the address of the page you request so it can send the page to you.

What we don’t do

There are no accounts, forms, or ads, and no advertising trackers. Links to other sites don’t tell them you came from here. The “Write to” buttons open your own email app; nothing is sent through this site.

If someone may be checking your device

Any website you visit can appear in your browser history. A private or incognito window keeps pages out of it. On the survivor services page, “Exit this page” goes straight to a weather site, but it does not erase your history.
