What Cornell could fund in Ithaca

Each page is one proposal. Every figure is footnoted to a published source.

  • The wastewater treatment plant

    Cornell says it could not operate without Ithaca’s wastewater plant. Its endowment paid out $439 million in fiscal 2025. One option: a gift for the plant’s repairs.
  • 9 to 5

    Survivor services

    Cornell’s own advocates work weekdays, 9 to 5. After hours, Cornell refers survivors of sexual violence to an independent local hotline. One option: a voluntary gift to that service.
